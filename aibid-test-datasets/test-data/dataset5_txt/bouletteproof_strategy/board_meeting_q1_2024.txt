ITEM 2 — CEO REPORT — Q1 2024 PERFORMANCE SUMMARY
================================================================================
Mutabazi Augustine presented the Q1 2024 performance highlights.

FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE (Q1 2024 vs Q1 2023):
• Total Revenue: $4.64M (+21% YoY) [AIBID Platform $1.66M; Custom AI $832k; Data Consulting $638k; Training $355k; Gov Tech $888k]
• Gross Margin: 80.1% (up from 77.6% in Q1 2023)
• EBITDA: $2.83M (margin: 61%) — highest quarterly EBITDA in company history
• ARR: $18.6M (milestone crossed; on track for $25M ARR by year-end)
• Active Clients: 275 (vs 175 in Q1 2023, +57%)
• Net Revenue Retention: 118% (all-time high; driven by BK Group 3-year renewal at +25%)

KEY WINS SINCE LAST BOARD MEETING:
1. KCB Bank Kenya: $520k, 3-year contract signed. Strategic win following I&M Bank acquisition.
2. African Union Commission: $880k, flagship institutional deal. AUC Chair visited Kigali HQ in person.
3. Jordan Ministry of Planning: $180k pilot — first Middle East contract; MENA entry confirmed.
4. Vodacom Tanzania: $460k, enterprise deal secured through CRDB referral.

KEY RISKS FLAGGED:
1. Equity Bank Rwanda renewal at risk (new CDO unfamiliar with platform). Meeting scheduled for April.
2. COGEBANQUE user adoption is low (60%) — intervention team deployed.
3. Airtel Rwanda renewal uncertain — Bharti Group vendor review in progress.

Board Discussion: Ms. Priya Mathur (Partech) raised concern about client concentration — top 5 clients represent 28% of ARR. CEO acknowledged this and noted geographic diversification as mitigation. Mr. Kofi Boateng (DFC) asked about the MENA pipeline; CEO confirmed three additional conversations underway in Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

RESOLVED: Board noted the Q1 2024 performance report with satisfaction. CFO to prepare sensitivity analysis on client concentration risk for Q2 board.

================================================================================

MTN RWANDA RESULTS (evidence base):
• 14.3% churn reduction confirmed by A/B test (3-month controlled study)
• ARPU increase of 8% attributable to AI-driven retention interventions
• 145 active platform users across analytics, network ops, and MoMo business units
• MTN Rwanda 3-year renewal signed at 20% price increase

MTN GROUP CONVERSATION:
• MTN Group CDO (Johannesburg) briefed by MTN Rwanda VP Data in February 2024
• MTN operates in 19 African countries with 290M subscribers
• Estimated group contract value: $4.2M – $6.8M ARR (depending on tier per country)

ITEM 6 — IPO PREPARATION UPDATE (RESTRICTED DISTRIBUTION)
================================================================================
Xaverine Mukankubana and CEO presented.

CONTEXT: The board previously approved exploring a dual listing on the Rwanda Stock Exchange (RSE) and London Stock Exchange (LSE) AIM market by 2026. This item covers the preparatory steps underway.

STATUS:
• PricewaterhouseCoopers Rwanda engaged as IPO readiness advisor (January 2024)
• Legal counsel: Bowmans Rwanda (local) and Linklaters (international)
• Target EBITDA for IPO year: $12M+ (projected for 2025 at current growth rate)
• Revenue target for IPO: $35M+ ARR (projected for 2026)
• Key pre-IPO risks: client concentration, geographic revenue mix, regulatory risk in some markets

PwC ASSESSMENT SUMMARY:
1. Financial controls are "IPO ready" — IFRS compliant since 2022.
2. Governance structure needs two additional independent board members.
3. Share option plan for staff needs to be formalised and documented.
4. Rwanda Stock Exchange expressed strong support — first tech company IPO would be historic.

RESOLVED:
1. Board mandates appointment of 2 independent non-executive directors by Q3 2024 — Nominations Committee to start search immediately.
2. Employee Share Option Plan to be formalised by Jeanne d'Arc Mukashema by Q2 2024.
3. Continue IPO preparatory activities under confidentiality — no public disclosure until H1 2025.

ITEM 8 — RISK REGISTER REVIEW
================================================================================
Top 5 Risks Reviewed by the Board:

1. TECHNOLOGY RISK — Generative AI model costs: Anthropic API costs have risen 28% since Q3 2023. Mitigation: explore model distillation and fine-tuning to reduce inference costs.

2. REGULATORY RISK — Data protection: Kenya Data Protection Act enforcement began January 2024. Tanzania is drafting similar legislation. Rwanda's existing Data Protection Law is compliant. Mitigation: Legal team conducting compliance audit for all East African jurisdictions.

3. COMMERCIAL RISK — Client concentration: Top 5 clients = 28% of ARR. Mitigation: actively diversifying across 12 sectors and 8 countries.

4. PEOPLE RISK — Key person dependency: CEO and 3 co-founders are critical. Mitigation: succession planning initiated; key person insurance being reviewed.

5. GEOPOLITICAL RISK — DRC operations: Political instability in eastern DRC could affect Rawbank operations. Mitigation: contract includes force majeure clause; payments secured via escrow.

RESOLVED: Risk register noted. Legal team to present Kenya/Tanzania data protection compliance report at Q2 board meeting.
