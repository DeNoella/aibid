CALL SUMMARY — VERTEX SYSTEMS DEAL NEGOTIATION
Date: May 8, 2024
Participants: Morgan Lee (AE, AIBID), Cassandra Wu (Vertex CPO), Finn Lachlan (Vertex Legal)
Duration: 52 minutes
==========================================================

CONTEXT
-------
Vertex Systems ($320K deal, currently in Negotiation stage) is evaluating AIBID Enterprise alongside a competitor (unnamed, referred to as "the other platform" by Cassandra). Decision expected by end of May.

KEY POINTS DISCUSSED
--------------------
1. CONTRACT TERMS
   Finn pushed back on the standard 12-month commitment, requesting a 6-month initial pilot with an option to extend. Morgan communicated that 6-month contracts require VP approval and carry a 15% price premium. Finn acknowledged and said they would "probably just do 12 months if the pilot clause can be added." Morgan to escalate to VP Sales for a custom pilot amendment clause.

2. SECURITY & COMPLIANCE
   Cassandra asked pointed questions about SOC 2 Type II certification status. Morgan confirmed it is in place and offered to share the audit report under NDA. Cassandra also asked about data residency — Vertex operates in the EU and requires data stored in Frankfurt region. Morgan confirmed EU data residency is available on Enterprise tier.

3. COMPETITOR CONTEXT
   Vertex has been demoing "the other platform" for 3 weeks. Cassandra mentioned they find AIBID's reporting UI superior but have concerns about AIBID's native Salesforce sync (bidirectional sync is listed as "coming soon"). Morgan: "Expected GA in Q3 2024 — I can get engineering to confirm timeline." This is a potential blocker.

4. PRICING
   The $320K figure is for 80 seats + full platform. Vertex asked about removing the AI Insights add-on to reduce cost. Morgan indicated that would bring price to ~$275K. Vertex seemed receptive.

NEXT STEPS
----------
- Morgan Lee: Escalate pilot amendment clause to VP Sales by May 10
- Morgan Lee: Share SOC 2 report (under NDA) by May 9
- Morgan Lee: Confirm Salesforce sync GA timeline with engineering
- Cassandra Wu: Internal alignment on pricing by May 15
- Morgan Lee: Follow-up call with full decision-makers May 20

DEAL RISK: Medium. Salesforce sync timeline is the swing factor.
